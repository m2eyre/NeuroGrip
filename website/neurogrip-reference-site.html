02 · How it works

From a thought to a grip,
in five steps.

No surgery and no sensors on the arm. A headset, a laptop and a $2 servo.

Clench detected → close EEG · Fp1 · 250 Hzt = 0 – 4.8 s
01

Intent

Clench your jaw or blink hard. The g.tec Unicorn headset reads it: 8 channels at 250 Hz.

02

Detect

Python filters the raw EEG and flags the clench when it crosses your calibrated threshold.

03

Command

A one-byte open or close command goes to the Arduino over USB serial.

04

Actuate

The Arduino Nano turns it into a PWM pulse for the SG90 micro-servo.

05

Grip

Two printed gear-fingers mirror each other: 2 mm closed, about 60 mm open.
